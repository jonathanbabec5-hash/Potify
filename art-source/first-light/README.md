# First Light at Canopy House

Open `Canopy_First_Light.blend` in Blender to edit the turn from the apartment window to Tessa Bloom. The scene includes its packed room image. The timeline runs from frame 1 through 25 at 30 frames per second.

The game holds a still until the player chooses to turn. It then plays the short rendered move and opens Tessa’s conversation. The hold, dialogue and audio fade are controlled by the game independently of the Blender timeline.
