# POTIFY CITY — PUBLIC BUILD-IN-PUBLIC CANON KEY

**$POTIFY: A Wonderful High Life** · v0.1.0 · November 4, 2026

A grounded, original 3D cultivation-and-city life simulator inspired by the daily rhythm and modest charm of early-2000s console farming games. Grow fictional crops, meet locals, explore a living city, build better facilities and discover stories hiding in everyday places.

**Concept and development registry, not a release announcement.** `LOCKED` is founder-approved creative direction; `DESIGNED` is specified but may not be implemented; `PROPOSED` is under consideration; `ART-ONLY` comes from illustrations; `VERIFIED` is a limited checked fact. No public launch date or console/Steam release has been announced. In-game goods and `simSOL` are fictional and nonredeemable; the real Solana token is separate. No real-world cannabis sales or investment returns.

**Style direction:** earthy, quietly charming, practical, original GameCube-era 3D life simulation; not pink chibi fantasy. The dog Shylo is an occasional supporting companion.

Follow verified development updates, suggest an NPC, describe a town activity, propose a useful game mechanic, or report a tested issue. New proposals do not become locked canon until approved and tested. No concepts should be presented as shipped solely because they appear in art or a feature registry.
