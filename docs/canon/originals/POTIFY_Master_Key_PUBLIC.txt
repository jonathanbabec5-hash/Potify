# $POTIFY: A Wonderful High Life — public world bible & development roadmap

**Build in public · Concept and roadmap · 4 November 2026 · Version 1.0**

A grounded, original 3D cannabis-themed city-life simulator drawing on the *feel* of early-2000s farming games: daily routines, slow progress, relationships, changing seasons, meaningful upgrades, and the occasional odd discovery. We are exploring a walkable city, a small growing plot, fictional plant genetics, residents with schedules, a market, a virtual economy, and lore connected to **The POTIFY Chronicles**.

**Status:** The project is under development. Descriptions below are goals or concepts unless independently confirmed in a playable build. Concept art is not gameplay. No storefront platform release has been announced.

**Virtual economy:** All harvest and shop transactions occur in the fictional game world. In-game **simSOL** and virtual **POTIFY harvest units** have no cash-out, redemption, token entitlement, or real cannabis purchasing function. This game does not constitute a new native blockchain or guarantee actual crypto rewards.

**What we're prioritizing:** believable GameCube-era atmosphere, physically interactive farm chores, a complete small first-day-to-first-harvest loop, reliable saves, and town residents worth knowing. Neon-overloaded and bright pink/chibi promotional looks are not the art direction.

## 01 · Game identity, pillars and boundaries

- **ROOT-001 — Working game title.** $POTIFY: A Wonderful High Life; short universe label POTIFY City, with title treatment of A Wonderful High Life. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-002 — Central genre.** Original cozy 3D farming/life simulator rooted in early-2000s GameCube-era pacing and controls, set in a fictional cannabis-growing city. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-003 — Inspirational lineage.** Emulate the feeling of Harvest Moon: A Wonderful Life and some Animal Crossing social exploration; do not reproduce copyrighted code, maps, layouts, characters, music or art assets. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-004 — The city is the farm.** Small grow plot or rooftop greenhouse at first; a living neighborhood and progressively larger property replace the traditional inherited farm. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-005 — Daily life first.** In-world movement, chores, seasons, conversation, shopping, sleeping and meaningful passage of time drive gameplay. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-006 — Grow–harvest–sell–upgrade.** The repeatable backbone of the game: acquire fictional genetics and supplies, care for virtual plants, harvest, sell, expand. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-007 — Seed → Soil → Solana.** Canonical POTIFY motto may appear subtly on books, signs, murals, ending titles and the journal. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-008 — The city has history.** Small details should imply that the town existed before the player arrived; do not explain every mystery with exposition. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-009 — Wonder without glitter.** Calm, grounded, witty, inhabited; little magical moments are rare and earn their impact. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-010 — Shylo's identity.** Shylo is a gray/cream Norwegian Elkhound with pointed ears and a curled tail; ambient supporting companion, not the central mascot. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-011 — Fictional commerce.** The game does not sell, deliver, redeem or enable purchases of real-world cannabis. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-012 — No financial promises.** Gameplay achievements, lore and Easter eggs do not promise real crypto, price appreciation or holder rewards. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-013 — Work in public.** Publish honest visual concepts, feature progress and changelogs without leaking puzzle answers or calling concepts finished gameplay. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-014 — Clear feature status.** User requests, concept art, assigned Replit work and actually verified playable functions are different evidence levels. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-015 — No platform claims.** No Steam, Epic, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo or other distribution support can be claimed without a verified release plan/approval. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-016 — GLP separation.** GreenLight Pay is unrelated to the fictional game economy and never presented as payment processing or token utility for this game. [LOCKED]

- **ROOT-017 — The first scene.** Wake in a modest room, notice a written task list, step into early morning, and survey a neglected growing space. [DESIGN]

- **ROOT-018 — Emotional arc.** Player learns responsibility through failure, rebuilding, friendships, and incremental improvements—not fast-money fireworks. [DESIGN]

- **ROOT-019 — Game identity shorthand.** From one rooftop (or modest plot) to a living city, with a long-term chapter of stewardship rather than an instant empire. [DESIGN]

- **ROOT-020 — Final IP and game publisher structure.** Working title and design are set; trademark/rights, age ratings, official business ownership and platform agreements require separate verification. [OPEN]


## 02 · Visual direction, interface and presentation

- **ART-001 — GameCube-era mood.** Muted stylized 3D inspired by original A Wonderful Life rather than bright mobile anime or modern high-saturation remake aesthetics. [LOCKED]

- **ART-002 — Grounded colors.** Worn olive, hay, dirt brown, weathered timber, concrete, brick, washed blue, soft yellow daylight, restrained green. [LOCKED]

- **ART-003 — No pink-first city.** Avoid pink or lavender-dominated skies, giant cherry blossoms, pastel kawaii UI, glitter, neon everywhere. [LOCKED]

- **ART-004 — People look like residents.** Modestly proportioned original 3D characters, readable silhouettes, worn practical clothes, human expressions. [LOCKED]

- **ART-005 — UI serves the chores.** Real-time scene takes priority over menus; small tool/status HUD; only contextual dialogue and shop panels when needed. [LOCKED]

- **ART-006 — No poster-as-gameplay.** Splash artwork is not proof of a 3D world; screenshots should come from the actual game when called gameplay. [LOCKED]

- **ART-007 — Originalized reference.** Use user-approved calmer concept board as guiding composition, with actual game props and work replacing decorative UI panels. [LOCKED]

- **ART-008 — Third-person camera.** Slightly above-and-behind follow camera, gentle perspective, manual turn and restrained distance options. [DESIGN]

- **ART-009 — Weathering and aging.** Wood chips, weather stains, dusty shelves, slightly crooked signs, used tools, creek stones, weeds between paving stones. [DESIGN]

- **ART-010 — Interior lighting.** Greenhouse daylight and subtle magenta grow lights only where justified; softer shadows indoors. [DESIGN]

- **ART-011 — Sky and clouds.** Blue-gray mornings, warm amber evenings, genuine dusk; cloud coverage changes by weather. [DESIGN]

- **ART-012 — Plant silhouettes.** Obvious visual difference between small vegetative plants, flowering plants and harvest-ready crops. [DESIGN]

- **ART-013 — Shopfront typography.** Painted shop signs and parchment-style tooltips; not huge hovering branded signs on every corner. [DESIGN]

- **ART-014 — Journal graphics.** Handwritten notebook, worn paper tabs, pressed-leaf bookmarks and map annotation stickers. [DESIGN]

- **ART-015 — Map design.** Hand-drawn village/district sketch with discovered locations; no false claims that all neighborhoods are unlocked. [DESIGN]

- **ART-016 — Character portrait restraint.** Optional dialogue portraits, original design, no oversized chibi eyes. [DESIGN]

- **ART-017 — Visible domesticity.** A sink, bed, dirty boots, calendar, kettle, hallway, shelf and meaningful storage. [DESIGN]

- **ART-018 — Gentle animations.** Breathing, head turns, routine idle behavior, watering, sorting harvests, sweeping, opening storefronts. [DESIGN]

- **ART-019 — An imperfect town.** Trucks, billboards, fences, service alleys, little hill trails and quiet water rather than an amusement park. [DESIGN]

- **ART-020 — Visual reward cadence.** Big reveal for a building upgrade or first harvest; everything else subtle. [DESIGN]

## 08 · Fictional economy and business progression

- **ECON-003 — In-game harvest units.** Harvest output is numerically tracked as virtual POTIFY-themed inventory units; cannot be redeemed for real tokens or products. [LOCKED]

- **ECON-004 — No real money checkout.** Shops and markets exchange only fictional resources for simSOL; no direct real cannabis transaction or actual token transfer. [LOCKED]

- **ECON-005 — Starter balance.** Initial candidate balance 120 simSOL, subject to tuning and testing. [DESIGN]

- **ECON-006 — Starter capacity.** Four plots, upgradeable to six/eight and larger facilities. [DESIGN]

- **ECON-009 — Baseline yield.** Candidate 4 virtual harvest units per plant at baseline grade. [DESIGN]

- **ECON-010 — Base sell price.** Candidate 8 simSOL per harvest unit, with supply/demand and grade modifiers. [DESIGN]
