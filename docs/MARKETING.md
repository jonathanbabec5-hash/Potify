# From a first harvest to a returning player

**Audience.** Adult cozy-game players who enjoy neighborhood stories, plus game developers interested in persistent browser worlds.

**Offer.** Free playtest of the first rooftop lease. The proposed paid product is an optional digital city field guide with approved maps and art. Set a price and offer fulfillment only after a sample and rights review. Game progression remains available in the free test.

| Window | Public proof and channel | One next action | Proposed target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | A captioned phone clip of planting, then meeting the first neighbor, linked from the game devlog | Start the free playtest | 10 unique starts |
| Week 2 | A short annotated diff showing how a returning grower keeps a legacy save | Complete one harvest and give feedback | 5 observed completions |
| Week 3–4 | A field-guide sample showing the rooftop floor plan and one resident's story | Register interest in the guide | 3 distinct opt-ins |

Chance owns the pilot and approves any outreach. Use a tagged link for the specific clip. Count opt-ins within seven days, deduplicate participants, and report returning players separately. A later sales test needs a listed price, a delivered file and a reconciled order after refunds. Until then, revenue and conversion baselines are unknown. Proposed cash spend is $0.
