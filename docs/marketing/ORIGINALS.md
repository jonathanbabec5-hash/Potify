# A Wonderful High Life · original marketing and design archive

Chance’s November 4 concept puts the player on a rooftop with a first crop and a neighborhood to explore. The two original social graphics show how that premise was introduced. Four days later, the game’s campaign draft asked players to choose between a bigger greenhouse and a delivery operation after their first harvest.

## Original public design materials

- [Public Master Key v1.0 (PDF)](../canon/originals/POTIFY_Master_Key_PUBLIC.pdf) and [its original plain-text companion](../canon/originals/POTIFY_Master_Key_PUBLIC.txt). These describe the public design for POTIFY City, including plans and open decisions. The public registry contains 469 entries.
- [Complete public website ZIP](../canon/originals/POTIFY_CITY_COMPLETE_PUBLIC_WEBSITE.zip) and [standalone offline master key](../canon/originals/POTIFY_City_Master_Key_Offline_PUBLIC.html). To browse offline, extract the ZIP and open its `index.html`.
- [Earlier public build log](../canon/originals/POTIFY_PUBLIC_BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_v0.1.md), [interactive public game bible](../canon/originals/POTIFY_PUBLIC_INTERACTIVE_MASTER_KEY.html) and [public build-log starter ZIP](../canon/originals/POTIFY_City_Public_Build_Log_Starter.zip). These preserve the earlier design language and original packaging.
- [Companion canon repository](https://github.com/jonathanbabec5-hash/potify-city-canon/tree/5628e8b1e12d19ec1f8e80a2f3c0a7a00b02ea8b) contains the searchable public source, including the JSON registry and CSV index.

[SHA-256 checksums](SHA256SUMS) cover the original files linked above. From the repository root, run `sha256sum -c docs/marketing/SHA256SUMS`. The [game README](../../README.md) identifies the playable baseline and the Roof 7B story branch.
